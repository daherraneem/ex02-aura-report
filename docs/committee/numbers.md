## 2. Unit Economics, Launch P&L & Break-Even (F1 — Chapters 4 & 5)

- **Israeli VAT Rate:** **18%** (effective Jan 1, 2025; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries, `pwc2025israelvat`).
- **Consumer List Price (incl. 18% VAT):** **69.0 ILS** per 10 ml unit; **299.0 ILS** for 5-shade D2C bundle (59.8 ILS/unit).
- **Net Revenue per Standard Unit (excl. 18% VAT):** `69.0 / 1.18` = **58.5 ILS**.
- **Variable Cost per Unit (labelled planning assumptions):**
  - Direct production COGS (formula, tube, carton): **14.0 ILS**
  - Payment processing & net fulfillment/shipping per unit: **6.5 ILS**
  - Blended channel commission (20% marketplace commission × 35% volume share; 0% on D2C & Tasting Bars): **4.0 ILS**
  - **Total Variable Cost per Unit:** **24.5 ILS**
- **Blended Contribution Margin per Unit:** `58.5 − 24.5` = **34.0 ILS/unit** (**58.1%** of net revenue).
- **Fixed 6-Month Launch Budget (`WP1`–`WP6`):** **150,000 ILS** (excludes founder/student-team salaries; initial inventory working capital of 3,000 units × 14 ILS = 42,000 ILS is recovered through COGS as units sell):
  - **52% (78K ILS):** Content/ASMR + 20–30 micro-influencers (43K) + Paid media, retargeting & surveys (35K)
  - **27% (40K ILS):** 3 Campus Tasting Bars (~13.3K ILS/event incl. booth, licensed catering, staff, 1,500 sealed mini samples)
  - **21% (32K ILS):** D2C website, Flavor & Shade Quiz & marketplace onboarding (22K) + Regulation `REQ-01` & trademark check (10K)
- **Break-Even Point (covering the 150K ILS launch budget):**
  - **`4,412` physical units** (`150,000 / 34.0`)
  - **`~304K` ILS gross consumer sales** (**`~258K` ILS net of 18% VAT**)
  - **Payback timing:** **Months 9–10** (after ~6–7 active selling months), yielding **+101.6K ILS** operating contribution at full Year-1 SOM (7,400 units).
