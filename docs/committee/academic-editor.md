1. **AE-01 · critical** — **Problem:** The central sales target (255K ILS, ~3,700 units) does not follow from the SOM it is "derived" from. Ch. 4 sets the year-1 SOM at ~511K ILS, then sets the launch target at ~255K ILS, which is 50% of the annual SOM. But sales only run in months 4–6, so half a year's revenue has to come in 3 months (25% of the year), from a brand that starts with zero awareness. The text still calls this "conservative". The time window is also described two ways: "ב-6 חודשי ההשקה" (ch. 4 table) and "סה"כ יעד 6 חודשים" (ch. 5 table) vs "בחודשים 4–6" (ch. 2, ch. 5 intro). "יחידות מקבילות" (= revenue ÷ 69) is never defined. Ch. 5 also routes 50% of revenue through D2C at a 115 ILS AOV that includes 299-ILS five-shade bundles (59.8 ILS/unit), so physical units ≠ 3,700. **Where:** `04-market.tex:89-94`, `:108` (table "כ-255 אלף ש"ח ב-6 חודשי ההשקה"); `02-problem.tex:49-51`, `:91`; `05-alternatives.tex:19-21`, `:267-269`, `:283`, `:298-302`; body pp. 4, 18–19, 28–29. **Concrete fix:** In ch. 4, add one explicit line that derives the launch target from the SOM (e.g. a monthly run-rate × 3 active months, with a ramp assumption), or lower the target. Use one wording everywhere ("בחודשי המכירה 4–6"). Define "יחידה מקבילה = הכנסה ÷ 69 ש"ח" at first use, or report real units by channel.
