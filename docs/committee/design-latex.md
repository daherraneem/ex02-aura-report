# Committee review — Design and LaTeX Expert

Scope: layout, typography, RTL/BiDi correctness, figures, tables, boxes, bibliography and `aura-report.cls`, based on all 50 pages of `main.pdf` (body page n = PDF page n+7), `main.log`, `main.lof`, and the sources in `chapters/`, `figures/` and `aura-report.cls`.

1. **DL-01 · critical** — **Problem:** In the List of Figures and List of Tables, every space after a `\ltr{}`/`\en{}` token is lost, so words run together and some phrases are scrambled: "לאורך 6חודשים", "AURAבחלופה", "SMARTלהשקה", "AURAבישראל", "150אלף ש"ח", "M1–M6מסומנות", "(0–2.5לכל קריטריון", "AURA(R = P × I". **Root cause:** `\en` and `\ltr` are fragile `\newcommand`s that end in `\aura@peekcolon` (`\futurelet`). In a moving argument (a caption) they expand into `.lof`/`.lot` as `…\futurelet \aura@next חודשים`. When that file is read back, TeX drops the space after the control word `\aura@next`. You can see this in `main.lof`, entry 4.1. **Where:** PDF pp. 5–6 (front pages ד–ה), entries 4.1, 5.2, 6.1, 6.2, 7.1 and 2.1, 2.2, 4.1, 4.3, 5.2, 6.1, 7.1. Cause: `aura-report.cls` lines ~138–139. **Concrete fix:** In the class, make both commands robust: `\DeclareRobustCommand{\en}[1]{…}` and `\DeclareRobustCommand{\ltr}[1]{…}`, or `\NewDocumentCommand`. Alternatively, give every affected caption a short optional argument without `\ltr`/`\en`. Raise the class version, then recompile `tests/bidi-test.tex`.

2. **DL-02 · critical** — **Problem:** Bibliography URLs run past the right margin, and one runs off the paper. Ref. [44] is 105 pt overfull ("theinfluencermarketingfacto…" is cut at the page edge). [5] is 47 pt over, [28] 25 pt, [3] 24 pt, and [1], [17], [41] are 4–8 pt over. Other URLs are letter-spaced to fill the line ("https : / / www . glossy . co / beauty / …" in [2], [6], [13], [14], [15], [18], [39], [43], [48], [53]). **Where:** PDF pp. 45–49 (body 38–42). `main.log` has 15 overfull boxes from `main.tex` line 43 (`\printreferences`). **Concrete fix:** In `\printreferences`, set the list ragged right (`\raggedright` inside the `english` environment, or `\renewcommand*{\bibfont}{\raggedright}`). Add `\setcounter{biburlnumpenalty}{100}`, `\setcounter{biburlucpenalty}{100}` and `\setcounter{biburllcpenalty}{100}` so URLs can break anywhere, and `\Urlmuskip=0mu plus 1mu` to stop the stretching. Replace the 5-line percent-encoded Hebrew URL in [9] with a short landing-page URL.

3. **DL-03 · major** — **Problem:** `\en{}` tokens are hyphenated or broken at their own hyphen across lines, which the rules forbid: "Super-" / "Pharm Online", "AI Flavor-" / "Sommelier", "RACI (Respon-" / "sible, Accountable…", and "(REQ-" / "01, חודשים 1–3)". **Where:** Body p. 28 / PDF 35 (`05-alternatives.tex:256`), body p. 30 / PDF 37 (`05-alternatives.tex:345`), body p. 32 / PDF 39 (`06-workplan.tex:70`), body p. 33 / PDF 40 (`06-workplan.tex:144`). Also `05-alternatives.tex:82`. **Concrete fix:** In the class, stop hyphenation inside `\en` (for example, wrap the content in `\hyphenrules{nohyphenation}` or set `\hyphenchar\font=-1` inside it). For short IDs (REQ-xx, Super-Pharm), use a non-breaking hyphen (U+2011) or `\mbox{}`. Locally, rephrase so the RACI expansion does not fall at the line end.

4. **DL-04 · major** — **Problem:** The two authors' tables are typographically inconsistent, and many are too small to read. Chapters 1–3 use `\small` (≈10.95 pt). Chapters 4–7 use `\scriptsize` (8 pt in a 12 pt document) for Tables 4.2, 5.1, 5.3, 5.4, 6.1, 6.3, 7.1 and 7.2, and `\footnotesize` for others. Table 6.2 (RACI) is normal size, right next to an 8 pt Table 6.1 in the same chapter. 8 pt text in dense multi-line cells is below print readability. **Where:** `04-market.tex:158`, `05-alternatives.tex:107,276,367`, `06-workplan.tex:51,130`, `07-risks.tex:33,75` (PDF pp. 27, 32, 36, 37, 39, 41, 42, 43). **Concrete fix:** Set one minimum, `\footnotesize` at most, preferably `\small`, for all tables. Move the size into a class-level table style instead of per-table switches. If a table does not fit, cut words, use landscape (`sidewaystable`), or split it. It must not shrink below 9 pt.

5. **DL-05 · major** — **Problem:** Text in the Python figures is illegible at print size. `decision_matrix_scores.py` draws at 9.6 in wide and is scaled to about 0.635 of that, so its labels (7.3–8.3 pt) print at about 4.6–5.3 pt. `gantt_launch.py` (9.5 in, ×0.63) prints its task labels (8.8 pt) at about 5.5 pt and its legend at about 5 pt. In-bar numbers in Fig. 5.1(א) are white on light honey and peach, with poor contrast. **Where:** Fig. 5.1, body p. 27 / PDF 34. Fig. 6.2, body p. 33 / PDF 40. **Concrete fix:** Set `figsize` to the real printed width (≈6.1 in for the 15.5 cm text width) and use fonts of at least 8 pt at that size. Then include the figure at `width=\linewidth`, with no down-scaling. Use dark text on the light segments.

6. **DL-06 · major** — **Problem:** Panel order in Fig. 5.1 is LTR: panel א is on the left and ב on the right. Fig. 4.1 correctly puts א on the right (its code says "read first in Hebrew RTL"), so the report contradicts itself. The Gantt chart (Fig. 6.2) also runs its time axis left to right with labels on the left, which goes against the RTL layout the rest of the document uses. **Where:** `figures/decision_matrix_scores.py:37` (`ax1` is the left axis). Body p. 27 / PDF 34. **Concrete fix:** Swap the axes (`fig, (ax2, ax1) = …`) so א is drawn on the right. For the Gantt chart, either invert the x-axis and put the task labels on the right, or state in the caption that the time axis runs left to right.

7. **DL-07 · major** — **Problem:** A product in a table is displayed backwards. `\ltr{211.6K} $\times$ \ltr{70\%} $\times$ \ltr{172.5}` consists of three separate LTR islands inside an RTL cell, so it reads "172.5 × 70% × 211.6K". It also contradicts Eq. (4.1), which shows the same SAM as 148,100 × 2.5 × 69. **Where:** Table 4.1, body p. 19 / PDF 26. `04-market.tex:107`. **Concrete fix:** Put the whole expression in one math or `\ltr{}` unit, for example `$211.6\text{K} \times 70\% \times 172.5$`, and use the same factorisation as Eq. (4.1).

8. **DL-08 · major** — **Problem:** Pages with large empty areas. Table 2.3 sits alone on a float page, vertically centred, with about 55% of the page empty. Body p. 15 / PDF 22 ends at Table 3.4 with about 35% blank, because §3.6 and its two unbreakable boxes are pushed to the next page. Body p. 15 / PDF 22 (`03-literature.tex:326–343`). Use `[t]` for Table 3.4 so the text after it can fill the page. Shorten the boxes, or allow the long `insightbox`/`definitionbox` to break (`breakable`) when it is the last element before a section. 9. **DL-09 · major** — **Problem:** In the LOF/LOT, numbers and text do not line up. Entries 5.1 and 5.2 are indented differently from 4.x and 6.x. Entry 4.2 is 54.6 pt overfull and sticks out past the right margin. Five more LOF entries and one LOT entry are 2–10 pt overfull. `main.log` also reports 117 "\endL or \endR problem" warnings, which point to unbalanced bidi nesting in moving arguments or headers. **Where:** PDF pp. 5–6. `main.log` lines ~1880–1935. **Concrete fix:** Fix DL-01 first, since the expanded `\LR`/`\futurelet` in `.lof` causes both problems. Then widen the number box (`\renewcommand*\l@figure{\@dottedtocline{1}{0em}{2.8em}}`, and the same for `\l@table`) and give long captions a short form `\caption[short]{long}`.

10. **DL-10 · major** — **Problem:** A TikZ diagram overlaps itself. In the launch-architecture diagram, the layer-title pills ("שכבה 2: גשר ההתנסות…" and "שכבה 3: המרה ואספקה…") sit on the borders of the inner boxes and partly cover them. The rotated dashed side label crowds the left frame. The figure also splits list item 1 of §5.3 across two pages. **Where:** Fig. 5.2, body p. 28 / PDF 35. `figures/tikz/launch_architecture.tex`. **Concrete fix:** Raise each layer frame's top padding by about 4 mm (or place the pill with `anchor=south` above the frame line), and move the side label outward by 2–3 mm. Place the figure `[t]` before the enumerate so the list is not interrupted.

11. **DL-11 · minor** — **Problem:** Numbers are separated from their unit, or from their Hebrew prefix, at line ends. 14. **DL-14 · minor** — **Problem:** The figure typography does not match the body. Matplotlib figures set everything in David, including Latin ("Peptide Lip Tint", "CAGR"), and use "₪…M". The body uses Cambria for Latin and Calibri/Gisha for captions, and writes ש"ח. The TikZ diagrams use the sans heading font. Use ש"ח consistently instead of ₪ in the figure labels.

15. **DL-15 · minor** — **Problem:** Box styles are used inconsistently across the authors' chapters. The "key message" peach box is used as a problem definition (Ch. 2), a knowledge gap (Ch. 3), and the closing message (Chs. 4–8). A ruby `warningbox` is used for a channel-conflict note (body p. 29 / PDF 36) that is not a risk warning. `definitionbox` holds a research question, a paper citation, a formula and term definitions. As a result, the colour no longer tells the reader what kind of box it is. **Where:** `02-problem.tex:68,81`, `03-literature.tex:266,343`, `05-alternatives.tex:150` and the box on body p. 29. **Concrete fix:** Write a box usage table into the class header comment. Define one semantic per box, and re-map the misused boxes: use `insightbox` for the channel-conflict note, and a plain paragraph or `insightbox` for the research question.

16. **DL-16 · minor** — **Problem:** Typographic quote marks and gershayim. Hebrew quotations use TeX English quotes (\`\`…''), which give English curly quotes (“גויאבה”, “מייצר בעצמו”). Abbreviations like ש"ח and ארה"ב use ASCII `"` instead of gershayim (״). The figures use ״ (`ש״ח`), so body and figures differ. **Where:** For example, `01-intro.tex:28`, `03-literature.tex` (many places), `05-alternatives.tex:337`. **Concrete fix:** Use straight Hebrew quotes or ״ consistently. Gershayim can be produced by a global replacement of `"` by U+05F4 inside Hebrew words.

17. **DL-17 · minor** — **Problem:** Front-matter and title details. The keyword line wraps so that a lone bullet and "אומניצ'אנל" start the second line (PDF p. 2). The TOC lists the abstract and abbreviations, but not the List of Figures or List of Tables. The English cover title has a doubled space after the colon ("AURA:␣␣A Food-Inspired…", PDF p. 1). The cover header's Hebrew course name ("יישומי AI ו-Vibe Coding במדע וביום-יום") does not match the English "AI Agents & Vibe Coding". **Where:** PDF pp. 1–3. `aura-report.cls` (`\keywords`, `\sep`). `main.tex`. **Concrete fix:** Make `\sep` `\unskip\nobreak\enspace\textbullet\ ` so a bullet never begins a line. Add `\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{…}` for the LOF and LOT. Remove the extra space in `\reporttitle`, and align the two course names.

18. **DL-18 · minor** — **Problem:** Hebrew in a formula is too small, and the equation numbering is uneven. In Eq.
